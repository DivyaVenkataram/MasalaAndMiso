About Us

We’re a food blog that cares about flavor, experience, and the stories behind the plate.

Masala & Miso is built around one idea: that the best restaurant writing goes beyond “was it good?” We focus on places recognized by the Michelin Guide—not to chase stars, but because those kitchens often push hardest on technique, sourcing, and narrative. We want to meet them with the same seriousness: honest about what works, curious about why it works, and always attentive to the cultural and human context of the meal.

We’re interested in how spice and umami show up across cuisines, how a dish carries memory and place, and how a single meal can feel like a story. Our reviews aim to be thoughtful and critical in the best sense—clear, specific, and grounded in what we actually tasted and felt.

Thanks for reading. We’re glad you’re here.
