A standout in our Bay Area visits. Thai and Californian flavors in harmony—polished, precise, and worthy of the star.

Reserve →

Nari holds one Michelin star and sits in a calm, modern space in Duboce Triangle. The cooking weaves Thai technique and ingredients with California’s produce and sensibility—nothing feels pasted on; the point of view is clear.

The balance of heat, acid, and sweetness in the main courses stayed with us. This is Thai-informed cooking that doesn’t lean on cliché.

Service is attentive without being stiff. The room works for both a serious dinner and a more relaxed meal. Portions and pricing feel fair for the level of execution.

We’ll add more detailed tasting notes and dish-by-dish impressions as we expand our review format. For now: Nari leads our current Bay Area ratings and is a place we’d return to without hesitation.
