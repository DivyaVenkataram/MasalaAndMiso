Best Of

Curated lists by city, experience, and budget—so you know where to book first.

Our Dining Philosophy

We believe the best meals are those where technique serves soul. Stars matter, but so do intention, ingredient integrity, and the unteachable sense of place. We seek restaurants that speak a clear culinary language—whether that's Edomae precision, French rigor, or California abundance—and we judge them by the same standards we bring from our training and our tables around the world.

Best by Experience

Best Omakase / Tasting

Le Bernardin (seafood), Alinea (theatre), Benu (multi-cultural). Each is a full narrative in one sitting.

Best Sushi

Sukiyabashi Jiro remains the reference. For something more accessible in the US, we'll point you to our rankings.

Best Garden-to-Table

Arpège (Paris) and Single Thread (Healdsburg). Both make the farm the star.

Best by Budget

Splurge ($$$)

French Laundry, Alinea, Le Bernardin, Arpège. Save up and go once. These are the landmarks.

Fine Dining ($$)

Atomix, Oriole, Lazy Bear, Narisawa. Two stars, slightly lower check—still exceptional.
